# Album 98 — In the Beginning Was the Word

Eight bilingual songs. In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. In the midst Jesus came, manifested in the flesh, received up in glory. At the end He comes again, and with the Lord one day is as a thousand years. Born of water and the Spirit. Walk according to the Spirit. The Comforter guides into all truth. Bear the fruit of the Spirit. Be filled with the Holy Spirit. Tamil original plus English cinematic companion. Sense kept.

## Tracklist
Song 01 — In the Beginning Was the Word — John 1:1; Psalm 90:2; Isaiah 42:5; Psalm 24:1

Song 02 — In the Midst Jesus Came — John 1; Philippians 2; Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 5; 1 Timothy 3:16

Song 03 — At the End Jesus — 2 Peter 3; Revelation 10:7; Isaiah 34:4

Song 04 — Born of Water and the Spirit — John 3:5–8; 1 John 3:9; 1 Peter 1:23; Genesis 1:2

Song 05 — Walk according to the Spirit — Romans 8:1–11

Song 06 — The Comforter, the Spirit of Truth — John 16:7–14

Song 07 — The Fruit of the Spirit — Galatians 5:22–25; 6:8

Song 08 — Filled with the Holy Spirit — Luke 1:15; 1 Corinthians 2:10; Ephesians 5:18; Titus 3:5–7; Acts 2:1–4

## Prayer and study
1. Prayer: Keep the Word first. Study: John 1:1 and Psalm 90:2 — He is from everlasting, before the mountains.
2. Prayer: Receive the One who came and will come again. Study: 1 Timothy 3:16 — manifested, justified, seen, preached, believed, received up.
3. Prayer: Do not call His patience delay. Study: 2 Peter 3:8–10 — one day as a thousand years. The heaven rolls like a scroll.
4. Prayer: Be born of water and the Spirit. Study: John 3:5–8 and 1 John 3:9 — flesh and blood do not inherit. The evil one does not touch the one born of God.
5. Prayer: Walk in the Spirit. Study: Romans 8:1–2 — no condemnation. The law of the Spirit of life sets free.
6. Prayer: Receive the Comforter. Study: John 16:7–14 — He convicts of sin, righteousness, and judgment, and glorifies Jesus.
7. Prayer: Sow to the Spirit. Study: Galatians 5:22–25 and 6:8 — crucify the desires. Reap everlasting life.
8. Prayer: Be filled, not drunk. Study: Ephesians 5:18 and Acts 2:1–4 — one accord, a mighty wind, the Spirit poured without measure.
